Portable PureLight adaptation

Thanks to Pedro Figueiredo, Zixuan Li, Beibei Wang, Miloš Hašan and Nima Khademi
Kalantari, "PureLight: Learning Complex Luminaires with Light Tracing", SIGGRAPH
Asia 2026, section 3.4, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04319.

The portable implementation adapts the released spline and receiver-flow design:
https://github.com/pedrovfigueiredo/purelight
Copyright (c) 2026 Pedro Figueiredo, Zixuan Li, Beibei Wang, Miloš Hašan,
and Nima Khademi Kalantari. Licensed CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en
Changes: independent NumPy/Slang inference, explicit FP32 MLPs with biases,
sparse zero-initialized dense grid, stable quadratic inverse, deterministic cap
frame, analytic-fixture training, source export instead of CUDA checkpoint layout.

Thanks to Thomas Müller and the tiny-cuda-nn authors for documenting periodic
quartic OneBlob encoding and multilevel dense-grid interpolation:
https://github.com/NVlabs/tiny-cuda-nn/tree/v2.0/include/tiny-cuda-nn
Their mathematical encoding definitions are independently implemented here;
no CUDA implementation code is redistributed.
