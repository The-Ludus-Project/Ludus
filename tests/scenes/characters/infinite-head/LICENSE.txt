Changes made for Ludus:

- infinite-head.bin is the binary chunk of LeePerrySmith.glb with each
  TEXCOORD_0 v replaced by 1 - v. The scan's UVs put v = 0 at the bottom of
  the image, as three.js samples them; glTF puts v = 0 at the top.
- infinite-head.gltf is a new glTF 2.0 document over that buffer. It keeps the
  mesh's accessors, references the colour map and the tangent-space normal map,
  names the one material slot "Skin", drops the exported camera and lamp nodes,
  and scales the mesh by 0.02 so that it is about 17 cm wide in metres.
- infinite-head-lit.gltf is infinite-head.gltf over the same buffer and maps,
  with the head raised to stand on the ground plane and a warm key and a cool
  fill directional light (KHR_lights_punctual) for the skin benchmark.
- infinite-head-hair.gltf uses the same lit bust, with the original Ludus hair
  ribbon mesh (hair-cards.bin) and RGBA strand texture (hair-cards.png) added
  under the engine's MIT license. The original scan and maps retain CC BY 3.0.
- Map-COL.jpg and Infinite-Level_02_Tangent_SmoothUV.jpg are unchanged.
